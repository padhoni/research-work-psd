Figure. Simulated outcomes from the EPIC research prototype. (A) Paired documentation times for five simulated clinicians from Proof of Concept.docx. (B) Mean pre- and post-intervention documentation times of 5.11 and 4.16 minutes, respectively, representing an 18.59% relative reduction. (C) Mean paired reduction of 0.95 minutes (95% Student t CI 0.888–1.012); two-sided paired t(4) = 42.485, p = 1.8348 × 10⁻⁶. (D) Synthetic CDS override rates of 89.37% and 33.15% generated using NumPy default_rng with seed 42, 10,000 alerts per group, and input probabilities of 0.89 and 0.34. Error bars in D are Wilson 95% confidence intervals. All data are simulated; these results do not establish clinical effectiveness or causality. NASA-TLX is omitted because the program has no default validated workload dataset.
