Fig. X. Simulated CDS alert override rates. Two groups of 10,000 hypothetical alerts were generated using assumed override probabilities of 0.89 and 0.34 and NumPy default_rng with seed 42. The generated rates were 89.37% (8,937/10,000) in the legacy CDS scenario and 33.15% (3,315/10,000) in the AI-assisted CDS scenario, a difference of 56.22 percentage points. Blue error bars represent Wilson 95% confidence intervals of 88.75%–89.96% and 32.23%–34.08%, respectively, assuming independent alerts. These differences reflect the input assumptions and do not establish a reduction in alert fatigue or an improvement in clinical safety. No deployment timeline or proactive interaction outcomes were modeled.
