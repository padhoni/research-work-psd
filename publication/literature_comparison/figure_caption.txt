Figure. Documentation burden and burnout from distinct evidence sources. (A) Project simulation: mean documentation time in 5,000 synthetic clinician pairs was 5.100 minutes before and 4.153 minutes after the simulated intervention, an 18.58% relative reduction. The synthetic generating assumptions determine these results; they are not observed clinical effects. (B) External published study: burnout was reported by 28/51 (54.9%) respondents before and 17/51 (33.3%) after digital-scribe use, a decrease of 21.6 percentage points using the reported rounded percentages. This is a separate cohort and is not an outcome measured by the project. Bars are descriptive summaries without inferential error bars. The panels do not establish that documentation savings caused a reduction in burnout.

Panel B source: Effect of a generative artificial intelligence digital scribe on pediatric provider documentation time, cognitive burden, and burnout. JAMIA Open, 2025. PMID: 40620477. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40620477/

Placement: the combined figure is appropriate only as an explicitly labeled simulation/literature comparison (e.g., Discussion). For a figure reporting only this project's Results, use documentation_simulation_only and state that burnout was not evaluated.
